# Martian Survival Guide – Research Hub

Welcome to our shared research repository for **NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 – Interplanetary Survival Guide: Martian Map**.
This repository is a common space for our team to collect and share everything we research throughout the project.

### What can we share here?
- Research about Mars and Jezero Crater.
- NASA datasets, images, and scientific findings.
- Useful articles, research papers, and documentation.
- Ideas, references, and project inspiration.
- Findings about Martian terrain, environment, and survival.
- Anything else relevant to our project.

### How to contribute
Everyone on the team is welcome to upload their research, add useful links, and share their findings.

**A small request:** Include the source link whenever possible so everyone can explore and verify the information.

Let's keep all our research in one place and learn from each other's findings!
